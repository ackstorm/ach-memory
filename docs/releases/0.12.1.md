# 0.12.1

- **Project pins are capped at 2,500 characters**, down from 3,500. With both
  caps full there is still room in the session start for titles in the index.
- **The purpose is cut at 600 characters**, at its last sentence end, so a long
  purpose never pushes the family lines or the totals line out of the session
  start.
- **A family list draft gives up after 20 seconds** instead of 120. It runs
  inside the retain that reaches 30 memories, so a slow gateway held that
  retain for up to two minutes.

Server change; a patch inside the 0.12.X range rolls out with no GitOps
change. A bank with more than 2,500 characters pinned gets the rest left out
of its session start, newest first, until something is unpinned.
