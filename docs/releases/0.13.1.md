# 0.13.1

- **The index fills the session start.** Each family line starts at 3 titles,
  as before; while the whole still fits the 8,000-character budget, every line
  gets one more. On production banks (2026-11-16): ach-memory shows 66 of 66
  titles (24 before), ach-runtime 49 of 190 (38), ach-agent 63 of 409 (39).
- **Recall keeps the engine's order.** It used to re-sort Hindsight's hits by
  their semantic score, which threw away the reranker's ranking. With
  `scope="all"` the project and user banks now alternate by rank, project
  first.
- **No semantic floor.** `MEMORY_RECALL_MIN_SEMANTIC` and the chart's
  `recallMinSemantic` are gone: the 0.60 floor dropped Spanish hits the
  reranker ranks first.
- **`relevance` and a "nothing clear" note.** With `MEMORY_LLM_DECISION_MODEL`
  set (chart `config.llm.decisionModel`), one call scores recall's first 10
  hits: each carries `relevance`, and when the best is under 0.6 a `note` says
  first that no memory clearly answers. Nothing is dropped; a failed or slow
  call (2 s) answers without it. `read_events.relevance` (migration 0006)
  records each recall's best, to choose the cut later from real queries.

Measured end to end through the `recall` tool on a replica of three banks,
with Hindsight's decision reranker over 100 candidates: hit@5 0.97/0.97/0.90 in
English and 1.00/0.88/0.62 in Spanish, against 0.80/0.75/0.45 and 0.35/0.35/0.10
for 0.13.0 with the cross-encoder; the note came on 96-97% of queries with no
answer and 1-5% of the others.

Deploy: a server change with a migration. The reranker itself is Hindsight
configuration (decision model, 100 candidates), deployed through GitOps with
its own key; ach-memory needs a key with `ackstorm.decision` for `relevance`.
